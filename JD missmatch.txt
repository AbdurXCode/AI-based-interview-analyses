Job Summary:

We are seeking a skilled Junior Data Scientist with solid Python programming and analytical capabilities to support data-driven initiatives and infrastructure setup across projects. The ideal candidate will be responsible for configuring Azure environments, maintaining Python-based automation and analytics solutions, and delivering actionable insights through data analysis. This role requires collaboration with cross-functional teams and an understanding of operational environments, particularly within BPO or call center settings.
The successful candidate will leverage Python libraries such as Pandas, NumPy, and Scikit-learn to process and analyze data, while ensuring Azure environments are configured according to best practices and security standards. In addition, the role involves identifying operational improvement opportunities and contributing to workflow enhancements within BPO environments.
Candidates should have a strong foundation in data analysis, statistical methods, and cloud-based infrastructure, along with the ability to communicate complex findings to stakeholders in a clear and structured manner. Familiarity with Azure services, machine learning concepts, and emerging technologies such as Agentic AI will be considered an advantage.


Primary Job Responsibilities:

Azure Setup and Configuration:
Set up and configure Azure environments for various projects
Ensure compliance with Azure best practices and security standards
Collaborate with cross-functional teams to implement and maintain Azure infrastructure.
Programming Skills in Python:
Maintain Python scripts for data processing, analysis, and automation tasks
Utilize Python libraries such as Pandas, NumPy, and Scikit-learn for data manipulation and machine learning


Analytical Skills:
Conduct in-depth data analysis using appropriate statistical methods
Interpret complex data sets and derive actionable insights.
Present findings in a clear and concise manner to stakeholders.


Operational Experience in BPO/Call Center Environment:
Have experience working in a BPO or call center environment, either directly or as a support function
Understand the operational challenges and requirements of such environments
Contribute to improving processes and workflows within the BPO/Call Center
